# Strategy

The judge rewards, in order: a line actually told to a younger self; something
only this person's older self could say, drawn from a life actually lived; a
line that lands in one breath, in a real voice; a turn, image or small surprise
that stays after one reading; honest and specific enough to sting a little.

This entry is built on all five:

- Addressed directly: it opens with "Kid", a line spoken to the younger self,
  not a slogan for nobody in particular.
- Lived detail: selling nights for small coins is the grind of someone who has
  traded sleep for little money, night after night — not a motto anyone could wear.
- One breath: a single sentence, no explaining, no second sentence doing the work.
- Turn and image: "coins that vanish by morning" flips into "sleep is the only
  currency that compounds" — money language turned back on rest.
- Sting: it names a real regret (nights sold cheap) and the price is paid in
  the only currency that cannot be earned back.
