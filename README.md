Entry for the [One Line to Your Younger Self](https://make.fun/bounty/u-boothlord-one-line-to-your-younger-self-22e97f76) bounty on make.fun.

## The line

> Kid, stop selling your nights for coins that vanish by morning — sleep is the only currency that compounds.

One line, in English, that the writer would send back to their younger self: no explanation, no context, no second sentence.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the entry line exactly as written to `/output/entry.txt` (no trailing newline), after checking it is a single line of 1–200 characters.
